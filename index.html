Excellence

In the Asian Games,
India has 54 medals.
India is among the top 4 nations
that won 50+ medals.

Yet, India is in 9th place.

Because rank is based first on the number of golds won.

World recognises you when you are at the top of your game.

Raj asked,
Your wife told me,
That you work on Sundays.

Jay replied,
We have a review on Monday.
I prepare the report for it.

Raj asked,
Can't we automate this report?

Jay replied,
I never thought about it.

Jay was lucky. But you have to think about your work-life balance yourself.

CEO asked,
We invested in training.
I don’t see any change.

HR Head replied,
It’s only one week.
Change is never overnight.

CEO said,
There must be a little change!

HR Head replied,
Think of it like making popcorn.
Nothing happens for first 2 mins
Then it’s a full transformation.

Learning is like heat, steady and silent at first. But give it time, and results will begin to burst.

Uber is a good company.

I asked,
Why do you say that?
Does it pay more?

He replied,
It is not about pay.
When I want to go home.
It gives me a trip near my home.
That shows they care.

People appreciate it, when you truly care.
