About Harjeet

The HR leader who explains business with stories and sketches

Harjeet Khanduja is Senior Vice President, Human Resources at Reliance Jio, an author of ten books, a TEDx speaker, an inventor with four patents and a Professor of Practice. For three decades he has worked on one question. How do we make work better for the people who do it?

Then and now

Same stage nerves. Bigger rooms.

He first faced an audience as a student poet at IIT Roorkee. Today he speaks to leaders at conferences across India and abroad.

Career

Thirty years of building HR where growth was fastest

Each chapter began with a company about to grow very quickly, and an HR function that had to keep up.

  1. Tata Motors8 years

    Began his career here and led the first Kaizen implementation, which earned recognition from Mr Ratan Tata.

  2. Nicholas PiramalHR digitisation

    Led the digital transformation of HR, long before HR technology became a boardroom topic.

  3. Reliance RetailCore team

    Part of the core team that grew the business from 500 to 28,000 people.

  4. ConJoin Group (Anthelio)2009 · now Atos

    Helped scale the organisation from $6 million to $250 million, and won the NASSCOM Diversity Award three years in a row.

  5. Reliance JioSince 2012

    Built the leadership team and laid the foundation of the HR function. Has since built more than 30 HR products across hiring, onboarding, learning and employee experience, including AI powered recommendations, assessments and fraud detection.

The other side

Meet Sardar Tuktukहास्य सम्राट, the king of comedy

Long before the boardrooms, Harjeet was a Hindi hasya poet. He made his television debut on Doordarshan in 1997 as Sardar Tuktuk, and the name stayed.

  • 1997Won the poetry reality show हंसगोला, hosted by Arun Gemini
  • 1998Performed on Wah Wah on SAB TV
  • 2001Given the title Hasya Samrat
  • 2012One of the most popular poets on Wah Wah, Kya Baat Hai!
  • 2016Toured the United States and felicitated by the World Hindi Foundation
  • 2020Honoured with the Vishwa Hindi Jyoti Bhushan Samman

His poems appear in हंगामा एक्सप्रेस and other collections, on the poetry website Anubhuti, and in his own book Tuktuk Ki Rail.
