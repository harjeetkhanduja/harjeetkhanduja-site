Harjeet Khanduja

Senior Vice President, Human Resources at Reliance Jio, author of ten books, TEDx speaker and inventor with four patents. An alumnus of IIT Roorkee and INSEAD. Some call him the RK Laxman of Business for the way he sketches ideas. On stage he is also Sardar Tuktuk, a Hindi hasya poet since 1997.
