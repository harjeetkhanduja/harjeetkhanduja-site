How coaching works

A clear path from first call to lasting change

Engagements typically run for three to six months, shaped around the goals you set.

  1. Discovery call

    A short conversation to understand where you are, where you want to be and whether we are a good fit.

  2. Assessment

    A Predictive Index profile and conversations with your manager and peers to see how others experience your leadership.

  3. Coaching sessions

    Regular one to one video sessions with practical work between them, focused on the situations you face this month.

  4. Progress review

    A check against the goals you set, and a plan to keep growing after the coaching ends.
